FAQ

Questions Before You Come In

A few practical answers for first-time sellers. If you need something more specific, call or text.

Call 239-826-4882
What types of gold do you buy?

We buy gold jewelry, scrap gold, broken pieces, coins, and bullion across common karats, including 10K, 14K, 18K, 22K, and 24K. Value is based on precious-metal content after testing.

Do you buy silver?

Yes. We buy sterling silver jewelry, silver coins, flatware, bars, rounds, and other silver items with precious-metal value.

How is my gold evaluated?

Items are tested for authenticity and purity, then weighed. The offer is based on the metal content and current market conditions. You are not obligated to sell.

Do you buy jewelry with diamonds?

Yes. We evaluate the gold, silver, or platinum content of the jewelry. Diamonds and other stones are considered separately and depend on the stones themselves.

Do I need an appointment?

Call or text 239-826-4882 to arrange an evaluation. We can confirm timing and what to bring before you visit.

How is the value of my gold determined?

Value is determined by tested purity, weight, and current precious-metals market pricing. Stones, condition, and the form of the material can also affect the offer.

Do you buy coins and bullion?

Yes. We buy gold and silver coins, bars, rounds, and other bullion. Each item is evaluated on its metal content and current market conditions.

Where are you located?

We serve Southwest Florida from an Estero-area buying office, including Fort Myers, Bonita Springs, Naples, Cape Coral, Fort Myers Beach, and Sanibel.

How do I get started?

Call or text 239-826-4882. Bring the items you would like evaluated and a valid government-issued photo ID.
